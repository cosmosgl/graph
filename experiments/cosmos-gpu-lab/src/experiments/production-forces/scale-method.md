## Layout checks

Read fresh XY at ticks 0, 1, 4, 8, 16, 31, 32, 99 and 100. Check every public link ID and its ordered endpoints, every pin's original coordinates, the absent slot's two NaNs and finiteness elsewhere. Both packages must reproduce the input at tick 0. Adjacent ticks 31/32 and 99/100 measure one step of residual motion. Both layouts must move at tick 1 and move less in the final step than in their first step.

The global shape check keeps the existing hierarchical fixture's 5% budget on paired RMS position error and radius change, normalized to the WebGL layout radius at each checkpoint. To catch a whole neighborhood dispersing inside a globally similar layout, also require each community to retain at least the WebGL fraction minus one percentage point. This permits at most one additional leaf out of every hundred to leave its assigned neighborhood. Membership and the neighborhood radius come from the seeded generator before simulation. The denominator includes all free leaves originally assigned to that community.

Report RMS spread about each community's own centroid, hub displacement from its input, mean/p95/maximum incident link length, RMS spring-length error and collision overlap count and depth. These additional metrics are measured only. The spring target is the larger of the configured rest length and the sum of the endpoint collision radii. The spatial grid checks every unordered pair that can overlap, including pinned points. Report maximum penetration and RMS penetration among the overlapping pairs. All measurements use graph units, independently of zoom and painted point size. Keep full coordinates with the captures so a divergence can be localized to its first measured tick.

Pick probes use canvas CSS coordinates shared by both packages. Read the painted pixel and the public callback in each preview. A clear miss, an isolated point and a visible link establish known targets. Compare the paint at an initially occupied location again after stepping to check that geometry follows the moving positions.
